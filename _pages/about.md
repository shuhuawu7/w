    <h1>Shuhua Wu</h1>

    <p>
      Shuhua Wu is a Ph.D. student in Management Information Systems at the Fox School of Business, Temple University. Prior to joining Temple, she earned a master’s degree in Business Analytics and Project Management from the University of Connecticut and a master’s degree in Accounting from Drexel University. Before attending Temple, Shuhua worked as an IT analyst at Louisiana State University for two and a half years.
    </p>

    <p>
      Her research interests include health information technology, the social impact of IT, and digital platforms. She examines how technology adoption and public or platform-level policies influence outcomes at both the individual and societal levels, primarily using econometric methods.
    </p>

    <p>
      Shuhua has taught and assisted in courses on data analytics and is committed to inclusive, student-centered pedagogy.
    </p>
